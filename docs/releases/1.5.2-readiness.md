## Proposed announcement copy — not approved or posted

> I'm sharing sdr-grader, an open-source, deterministic linter for Adobe Customer
> Journey Analytics and Adobe Analytics snapshots. It produces a self-contained
> HTML report and JSON findings, with strict and pragmatic YAML rubrics. File
> grading runs locally without Adobe credentials or network calls.
>
> This is a public preview. Grades use provisional maintainer-judgment thresholds;
> they are review aids, not certification. The default catalog has 27 applicable
> checks per platform, and missing evidence is disclosed as not assessed. Review
> findings against your implementation before changing production configuration.
>
> Try the synthetic reports and installation guide:
> https://brian-a-au.github.io/sdr-grader/
> https://github.com/brian-a-au/sdr-grader

Claims permitted for review: provisional offline snapshot linting, versioned
rubrics, HTML/JSON reports, verified Ubuntu/Python 3.11 and 3.12 environments.
Do not claim empirically calibrated accuracy, a complete platform audit,
verified live Adobe workflows, or runtime serialization/product binding.
Live-generator and wider environment validation remain unverified launch claims.
