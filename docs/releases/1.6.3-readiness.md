## Generator marker validation

PR #151 recorded this evidence before merge. It uses aggregate counts only.

- The released `cja_auto_sdr` 3.13.6 and `aa_auto_sdr` 1.24.5 were installed
  from PyPI. Each produced a fresh snapshot from a live source, in the same
  form that `--dataview` and `--rsid` request. The previously tested 3.13.2 and
  1.24.1 produced snapshots of the same sources.
- File, directory, stdin, live and trend modes exited 0 on both platforms, and
  none printed a version warning after the change.
- AA: the `--json` reports for the 1.24.1 and 1.24.5 snapshots were the same,
  apart from the adapter version, under both packs.
- CJA: the segment definitions in the 3.13.2 and 3.13.6 snapshots were the
  same. The generator's own `nesting_depth` was greater for 11 of 103 segments,
  because 3.13.6 counts the steps of sequential segments. SEG-007 flagged 4
  segments instead of 2. In the pragmatic pack the segment complexity category
  went from 57% to 43%. The overall grade was F for both.
- The historical private compatibility corpus plus the fresh snapshots had 220
  entries (AA 18, CJA 202). All 220 graded with exit code 0, and none printed a
  version warning after the change. Real CJA exports do not declare a tool
  version, so the CJA marker applies only to snapshots that add one.
